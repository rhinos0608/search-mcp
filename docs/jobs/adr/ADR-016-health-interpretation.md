# ADR-016: Health and community interpretation

**Status:** Approved

## Context

Administrative, assistant, registered professional, and community roles have different stated requirements.

## Decision

Knowledge schemas interpret stated registration/check requirements; they never invent blockers. Missing registration evidence yields `registration_not_evidenced`, not automatic rejection. Clinical title similarity does not establish clinical regulation.

## Verification

Test administrative health, assistant, registered, disability/community, and missing-evidence cases.
